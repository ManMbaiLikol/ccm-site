The institution

Structuring the music sector, with all its players

A non-profit, non-political association, the Cameroon Music Council brings together musicians, arrangers, trainers, researchers, musicologists, critics, producers, sound engineers, broadcasters, publishers, promoters and copyright specialists.

Our mission

A driving force with national and international institutions

The CCM structures the music industry in Cameroon while ensuring that its players thrive. It supports creation, helps works reach audiences, encourages music education for young people, documents and promotes the heritage, and safeguards musicians' status and rights.

On the socio-economic front, it contributes to wealth and job creation, and advocates with the Ministry of the Economy, Planning and Regional Development for music to be included in the National Development Strategy 2020-2030 (SND30).

History

Born of the International Music Council

The CCM is the national association representing Cameroon in the network of the International Music Council, founded in 1949 at UNESCO's initiative.

  1. 2001

    At its General Assembly, the International Music Council proclaims the five music rights.

  2. 2003

    At the 30th IMC General Assembly, Cameroon is admitted as a member, following a reasoned application by Luc Yatchokeu, then president of the CIGALE association. The IMC asks for a dedicated national association to be created.

  3. 2009

    The Cameroon Music Council is founded under the aegis of Professor Lupwishi Mbuyamba, IMC regional coordinator for Africa. Registered in Douala on 14 September 2009 (no. 133/2009/RDDA/C19/BAPP).

  4. 2021

    Ordinary and extraordinary General Assemblies held in Yaoundé on 31 March: statutes amended and a new executive board elected.

  5. 2024

    The Cameroon Music Worksites bring together 64 professionals from all ten regions and lead to the Introduction to the Development Plan of the Cameroonian Music Industry.

  6. 2026

    Launch of the online directory of CCM card-holding members.

Our values

The five music rights

Proclaimed in 2001 by the International Music Council, they guide the CCM's work in Cameroon.

  1. 1

    The right for all children and adults to express themselves musically in all freedom.

  2. 2

    The right to learn musical languages and skills.

  3. 3

    The right to have access to musical involvement through participation, listening, creation and information.

  4. 4

    The right for musical artists to develop their artistry and communicate through all media, with proper facilities at their disposal.

  5. 5

    The right for musical artists to obtain just recognition and fair remuneration for their work.

Governance

A transparent, democratic association

The CCM's statutes were amended and its executive board elected at the General Assemblies held in Yaoundé on 31 March 2021.

The Council's bodies

General Assembly
Made up of all members.
Executive Board
A president, two vice-presidents, a secretary general and deputy, a treasurer, a communications officer and two operations officers, one in Douala and one in Yaoundé.
Music Observatory
Internal body providing technical evidence for the CCM's dealings with the Ministry of Arts and Culture, local authorities and other institutions. It submits an annual report to the executive board.
Committees
Finance committee, legal committee and committee supporting music initiatives.
Advisory committee
Oversees the proper running of the CCM in line with the vision of the International Music Council and the good practices recommended by national and international institutions.
Regional representatives
Individual or corporate CCM members who represent the association and relay its activities in their region.
